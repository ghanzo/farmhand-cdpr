The math

Towers hold cable ends at fixed points Ti. The camera sits at P = (x, y, z). Each cable’s length is just 3D Pythagoras:

Li = |P − Ti| = √( (x−xi)² + (y−yi)² + (z−zi)² )

When the camera moves with velocity v, each motor must pay out (or reel in) at:

dLi/dt = ( (P − Ti) · v ) / Li

The dot product projects the camera’s motion onto each cable’s direction — so a straight, constant-speed camera path demands four different, continuously changing motor speeds. Steppers approximate the ideal length with the v1 rule: take the next step only when it lands closer than staying put (error ≤ ½ step = 2.5 cm here). Cables are modeled straight — no catenary sag yet.
