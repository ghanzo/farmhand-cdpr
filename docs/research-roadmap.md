# Research roadmap

## Phase 1 — simulation foundation

- Validate rigid-body cable geometry and numerical conventions.
- Map tension-feasible workspace for a 10 × 10 metre pilot.
- Add cable velocity, reel, tower-load, and energy models.
- Define measurable requirements for scanning and targeted spraying.

## Phase 2 — bench mechanism

- Build a small indoor four-tower frame.
- Measure cable length, tension, repeatability, backlash, and settling time.
- Test a rigid vertical stage with a camera and spray surrogate.
- Compare measured behavior with the simulator.

## Phase 3 — outdoor pilot

- Instrument towers, foundations, cables, winches, weather, and carrier pose.
- Operate over a 10 × 10 metre plot above a low-risk crop.
- Demonstrate repeatable scanning and targeted liquid application.
- Quantify downtime, calibration drift, energy, and plant contact.

## Phase 4 — manipulation

- Add compliant local manipulation and force sensing.
- Trial sampling, pruning, delicate harvesting, and low-force weeding.
- Develop a bracing strategy before force-heavy soil engagement.

## Phase 5 — agronomic comparison

- Compare reduced-traffic and conventional management.
- Measure soil physical properties, biology, crop nutrition, health, yield,
  energy, labor, and cost.
- Publish raw data, calibration records, negative results, and uncertainty.

## Scale gate

Do not extrapolate directly from a 10 metre pilot to a hectare. Expansion
requires demonstrated structural scaling, wind behavior, cable dynamics,
position accuracy, safety, throughput, and economic advantage.
